Working Mystery Gift prototype

We have a working prototype of Mystery Event for an unmodded Switch or Switch 2 FireRed / LeafGreen game. Come to Pokémon Worlds / XP in San Francisco to get an exclusive look!

This was acheived by building off of tornadus's FRLG LDN trade project. By extending this project to support hosting trades and wireless adapter interactions, we can now send custom mystery gift events to stock, unmodded Switch 1 and Switch 2 FireRed / LeafGreen games with a simple linux and Wifi-USB adapter!

More details will come up as the team works on streamlining it so it is easy for anyone to try!
